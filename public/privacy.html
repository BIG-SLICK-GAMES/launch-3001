Launch3001 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Launch3001 stores game settings, checkpoint progress, scores, and leaderboard entries locally on your device using browser local storage.

Data Collection

This version does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information on a server.

Device Features

The game may request motion sensor access for tilt controls and VR/XR access for headset play. These inputs are used for gameplay only.

Future Online Features

If online leaderboards or accounts are added, this policy must be updated before release to explain what data is collected and how it is used.
